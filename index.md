This directory lists 42 people who are blind or have low vision and who lead in time-based media: podcasts, talks and keynotes, video, broadcast, and film. It is meant as a resource for aspiring blind presenters and as a way to find and reach the people listed.

This is one of three companion directories, alongside [Blind Authors](https://jamalmazrui.github.io/BlindAuthors/) and [Blind Developers](https://jamalmazrui.github.io/BlindDevelopers/), and all three are merged in [Blind Creators](https://jamalmazrui.github.io/BlindCreators/). Two related directories list works rather than people: [Blind Apps](https://jamalmazrui.github.io/BlindApps/) and [Blind Books](https://jamalmazrui.github.io/BlindBooks/).

A presenter is included here when every one of the following is true:

- The presenter is blind or has low vision.
- The work is contemporary, from the year 2010 onward.
- The work shows sustained, notable leadership rather than a one-off clip — at least one of: a keynote or featured address at a recognized conference or event; an established, regularly published series; a body of at least three published works; or professional standing such as a broadcasting role, festival selection, or major recognition.
- The presenter holds the role in their own right. Collaborative work counts, but everyone listed has at least one series or body of presentations created on their own.
- The presenter is credited with at least three public presentations of their own. Presentations made in collaboration with others can count in addition, but the three that qualify a presenter must be theirs alone.

Up to three works are shown per presenter, and each entry ends with a "Profiles:" line — a website, podcast, channel, LinkedIn, or Wikipedia, listed alphabetically by label.

Presenters are listed alphabetically by last name, with the works grouped by type in an appendix.

This directory is English-language in scope: it relies on talks, recordings, and sources that can be followed in English. Blind presenters who work in other languages are not represented here.

## Presenters (Alphabetical by Last Name)

### Kirk Adams {#pres-adams}
Blind since age five, a former president and CEO of both the American Foundation for the Blind and the Seattle Lighthouse for the Blind, now a professional keynote speaker on disability inclusion and accessible leadership.
Profiles: [Website](https://drkirkadams.com/)

#### [Keynote speaking on disability inclusion and leadership](https://drkirkadams.com/) {#work-adams-keynotes}
A sought-after keynote speaker at national and international conferences on accessibility, inclusive leadership, and resilience.

### Jeff Bishop {#pres-bishop}
President of Blind Information Technology Solutions (BITS) and an IT accessibility consultant at the University of Arizona, who has presented assistive-technology topics for the blind community on Tek Talk and at VIBUG since 2020.

#### [Jeff Bishop Discusses the GLOW Accessibility Toolkit (2026)](https://www.accessibleworld.org/podcast/tek-talk-welcomes-jeff-bishop-to-discuss-the-glow-accessibility-toolkit-that-helps-authors-publishers-and-organizations-produce-accessible-documents-05-04-2026/) {#work-bishop-glow}
His Tek Talk presentation on the GLOW toolkit, which audits, fixes, and templates Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Markdown, PDF, and ePub documents for accessibility.
